- PROP_MailClump Fan_B r3 (28 Oct 2026): sheets nested on one path through one U read as a single folded sheet; rule: for "a stack of mail", give each sheet its own free top edge where the camera sees it, stepped 5 to 10 mm and alternating sides, with leans growing toward the back so neighbours open apart; check the read in a gray Cycles crop, not only the outline.
- PROP_MailClump Fan_B Lean r4 candidate (28 Oct 2026): "rising one way" means every sheet turned the same sign, with its high corner on the outline's high side and the sheets in front stepping down; low fan angles cannot pinch a corner into a slot, so decide which sheets are held and which are tucked before solving widths; build_hash changes between identical rebuilds, so compare sorted vertices.
