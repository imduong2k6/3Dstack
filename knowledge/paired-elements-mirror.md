# One of a pair of doors sits off its recess

**Seen on:** corridor pilot, P2 to G3, Oct 2026 · **Modes:** all · **Routes:** all

**Symptom:** in the photo both doors sit symmetrically, each set into a recess. In the scene the left door stood proud of the wall, off its recess and not lined up with the wall's inset. The right door was right. The miss got through every later gate, because each review looked at what changed in that phase.
**Cause:** the two doors were placed separately, and no check compared them.
**Fix:** place one of a pair, then mirror it across the axis the reference shows. Model the recess in the wall shell, not as a separate frame in front of it.
**Test:** the supervisor's paired-element check at every placement review: each pair mirrors within 1 cm, and each piece's face sits at its recess depth (`supervisor`, Placement row).
